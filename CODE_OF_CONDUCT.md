# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the IntelliCircle community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**

- Being respectful, welcoming, and inclusive to all participants
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the IntelliCircle community and its users
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment of any kind
- Publishing others' private information, such as physical or electronic addresses, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. If you encounter unacceptable behavior, please reach out to the project owner or maintainers directly via their GitHub profile contact details.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of any incident.
